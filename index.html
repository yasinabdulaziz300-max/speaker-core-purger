Speaker tone tests

Each tone plays for 2 seconds. The tone is generated in full, but most phone speakers cannot reproduce 60–100 Hz, so those may be silent or only faintly felt. A rattle or crackle means the cone or grille is obstructed.

Stereo channel check

Before you start

Media volume changes must be made with your phone's buttons; a web page cannot raise system volume. Keep the speaker facing down over a soft cloth.

Sessions stop automatically. If you hear distortion or buzzing, stop and lower the volume. Tones are limited to 100–2500 Hz at a capped gain that drops automatically above 1000 Hz.
