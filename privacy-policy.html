Privacy Policy

Speaker Core Purger, by Kepza. Effective September 30, 2026.

Summary

Speaker Core Purger needs no account and no sign-up. We do not run a server, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob may collect some device data to do that, as explained below.

What the app does on your phone

The app plays sound through your speaker, can vibrate your phone, and shows details such as screen size, memory and battery level. This information is read and displayed on your device only. It is not sent to us or to anyone else by our own code.

Advertising

The app may show banner or full-screen ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this information from you directly, and we do not control how Google handles it.

  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings, under Google > Ads. Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom may be shown a consent message before personalized ads are served.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or precise location. The app does not use your microphone or camera.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data retention and security

Because we do not collect or store your data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Any data collected by AdMob is handled under Google's own policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.
